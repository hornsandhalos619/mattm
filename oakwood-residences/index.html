Designed for Those Who Demand More

Fine rentals city-wide — homes and apartments for rent. Oakwood Residences is a collection of 50 thoughtfully designed premium apartments in one of San Diego's most dynamic neighborhoods, with resort-style amenities that feel like they belong at a five-star hotel.

Every residence features floor-to-ceiling windows with panoramic views, chef's kitchens with integrated Miele appliances, wide-plank European oak flooring, and spa-inspired baths with rain showers. Smart-home automation, in-unit washer/dryer, and private balconies come standard.
