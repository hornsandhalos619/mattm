# Horns & Halos — First Collection Specification
**Season:** FW26 Core Drop | **Chapters:** I–III | **SKUs:** 15
**Brand Pillars:** Duality, Authenticity, Quality, Storytelling
**Fabrication:** 300gsm heavyweight cotton (tees), 380gsm fleece (hoodies), 100% cotton twill (caps)
**Color System:** Obsidian Black, Bone White, Burnt Gold, Blood Red, Charcoal, Stone Grey

---

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY: POLARIZED YET COMPLEMENTARY

> **"Chaos is honest. Order is a lie we tell ourselves."** — Chapter I: The Fall (Horns)
> **"Light doesn't erase darkness. It reveals it."** — Chapter II: The Rise (Halos)
> **"You are not one or the other. You are the space between."** — Chapter III: The Balance (Duality)

Each design exists in **tension with its counterpart** — same motif, inverted energy. The Horns version leans into distortion, aggression, rawness. The Halos version leans into symmetry, radiance, restraint. Together they form a complete emotional spectrum.
