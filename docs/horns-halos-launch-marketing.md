# Horns & Halos — Launch Marketing Sequence
**Brand:** Horns & Halos Streetwear | **Model:** $0 Paid Media, Organic/Word-of-Mouth
**Launch Window:** 8 Weeks (Pre-launch + 3 Chapters) | **Target:** 2.5-3.5% CVR, 15-18% Email Capture, 30%+ 90-Day Repeat
**Channels:** Email (Klaviyo), SMS (Postscript), Instagram/TikTok Organic, Discord Community

---

## STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK

### Core Philosophy (from Market Research)
> **"Status is derived from wearing brands people don't know about. Being part of a community that isn't widely known is the new driver of taste."** — Kelly Acheampong, Undiscovered (Vogue 2025)

> **"Authenticity is felt instantly... respect your references enough to understand them before you reinterpret them. Obsess over quality, because the details are what separate a real brand from merch."** — Tokyo Tengu Founder

> **"People connect to what we're doing... the visual representation has to be unique."** — BARRASERB

### Launch Model: Human Made x Parke Hybrid
- **Thursday Announce → Saturday Drop** (Human Made regulated rhythm)
- **Zero Paid Media** — organic/word-of-mouth only (Parke: $16M revenue, 0 paid media)
- **Pre-order Option** — no overproduction, trust-building (Tokyo Tengu model)
- **Story-First** — every drop = chapter continuation, not product dump

## IRL ACTIVATIONS (BARRASERB Model)

### Activation 1: "THE BUS" (Week 3-4, Chapter I)
- **Concept:** Rent a matte black sprinter van. Drive to 3 cities (LA, Austin, NYC or local equivalents).
- **Action:** Park at skate spots / colleges / art districts. Give away 50 Horns tees (H-02 "Flock Fractures" — the rebel sheep).
- **Condition:** No purchase. Just "Which one are you?" conversation. Photo for Discord.
- **Document:** TikTok/Reels series: "The Bus Tour"

### Activation 2: "LISTENING PARTY" (Every Drop Saturday)
- **Location:** Local record store / cafe / gallery (partner, no cost)
- **Format:** 2hr. Curated playlist (Chapter I: industrial/doom. Chapter II: ambient/gospel. Chapter III: experimental).
- **Perk:** Attendees get 1hr early shop access + exclusive sticker pack.
- **Stream:** Discord voice + IG Live simultaneously.

### Activation 3: "FIT CHECK WALK" (Week 7, Chapter III)
- **Concept:** Community walk — everyone wears their Horns/Halos/Duality pieces.
- **Route:** 2 miles through city center. End at park for picnic.
- **Doc:** Group photo = "The Balance" key art for next season.

### Activation 4: "GALLERY SHOWING" (Post-Launch, Month 3)
- **Concept:** Rent cheap gallery space. Display: original sketches, fabric swatches, rejected samples, chapter manuscripts.
- **Open:** Free entry. Merch table (Archive pieces only).
- **Press:** Invite local arts/culture writers (not fashion press).
