# Horns & Halos — Affiliate Program Structure
**Partners:** Fragrance, Eyewear, Home, Art, Wellness Brands
**Model:** Curated affiliate (not open network) | **Commission:** 15-30% | **Management:** UpPromote/Refersion
**Launch:** Week 9 (Post Chapter III) | **Integration:** Shopify + Klaviyo + Discord

---

## PROGRAM PHILOSOPHY

> **"We don't sell accessories. We complete chapters."**

This is not a generic affiliate program. It's a **chapter extension** — each partner brand occupies a specific narrative space in our three-chapter story. Partners are invited, not recruited. Commissions reward story alignment, not just traffic.

## COMMISSION RULES & GUARDRAILS

### Standard Rules
| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Self-referral** | Blocked (auto-detect same IP/email/household) |
| **Coupon stacking** | Affiliate code + sitewide code = commission on discounted total |
| **Returns** | Commission clawed back on full return (partial = pro-rated) |
| **Chargebacks** | Affiliate liable for fraudulent orders |
| **Brand bidding** | No bidding on "Horns & Halos" branded terms |
| **Discount abuse** | Codes monitored — bulk sharing = suspension |

### Tier-Specific Rules
| Tier | Min Payout | Cookie | Special |
|------|------------|--------|---------|
| **Brand Partners** | $50 | 30 days | Custom landing page option |
| **Collective** | Via Collective | Collective | Collective handles |
| **Creators** | $25 | 30 days | 30% commission, free product |

## LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

### Affiliate Agreement (Key Terms)
- **Independent Contractor** — No employment relationship
- **FTC Disclosure** — Mandatory: "#ad #HornsAndHalosAffiliate" on all posts
- **Brand Guidelines** — Must follow provided assets/copy (approval required for custom)
- **Data Privacy** — GDPR/CCPA compliant (no customer data shared)
- **Termination** — 30-day notice either side; immediate for fraud/brand damage
- **IP** — Affiliate gets license to use assets during term only

### Compliance Checklist
- [ ] Affiliate agreement signed (digital via UpPromote)
- [ ] W-9 collected (US) / W-8BEN (international)
- [ ] FTC disclosure language in welcome email
- [ ] Brand guidelines acknowledged
- [ ] Tracking test completed (test purchase → commission recorded)

## RISK MITIGATION

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| **Brand partner conflict** | Low | Medium | Chapter-exclusive alignment; no overlapping categories |
| **Creator inauthenticity** | Medium | High | Strict entry requirements; monthly auth checks; community reporting |
| **Commission fraud** | Low | Medium | UpPromote fraud detection; manual review >$500 orders |
| **FTC non-compliance** | Medium | High | Mandatory disclosure in agreement; automated reminders; audit sample |
| **Margin erosion** | Low | Medium | Blended commission cap at 25%; no discount stacking on affiliate orders |
| **Channel conflict (Collective)** | Low | Low | Clear separation: Collective = footwear/eyewear; Affiliate = fragrance/home/wellness |
